Batch attendance report

Attendance rate = present ÷ attendance records (one record per student per class). Use the Centres, Batches, Teachers and Subjects filters (several can be selected) to narrow the whole report, and click a batch to see its classes. Use Absent list on any centre, batch or class to see who was absent and their mobile numbers.

Generate report

Choose the Batch Attendance Report (.xlsx or .csv) or drop it on this box. It is read in your browser and is not sent anywhere. Then pick a start and end date to build the report for that period.
